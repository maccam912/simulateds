# Linden Grove 3D asset guide

Linden Grove is a sunny, lived-in neighborhood of small homes and ordinary
routines. Its models should feel like modest, well-loved household objects:
rounded upholstery, painted enamel, oak, cream plaster, terracotta and healthy
plants. Preserve the established palette in [DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md](DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md)
and `game/style.gd`. Shape and lighting carry the detail at the normal play zoom.

The first collection is original geometry authored for this project, generated by
[tools/generate_models.py](../tools/generate_models.py), and covered by the
repository's MIT license. No downloaded model, generated raster, photogrammetry,
or 2D-to-3D service is used. The GLBs are actual solid mesh geometry; plants and
people are not billboard planes. Existing UI portraits and preview illustrations
remain appropriate 2D interface artwork.

![The original Linden Grove model collection](../assets/models/gallery.png)

## Sources and regeneration

The source generator is the editable master. Commit it alongside changed GLBs
and [manifest.json](../assets/models/manifest.json). Blender is an authoring tool;
players and normal Godot builds load the committed GLBs without Blender.

```sh
# Blender on PATH (validated with Blender 5.1.2)
blender --background --python tools/generate_models.py -- --gallery

# Typical macOS application installation
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender \
  --background --python tools/generate_models.py -- --gallery

python3 tools/check_models.py
python3 tools/godot.py --headless --editor --import --quit
```

The optional `--gallery` renders a contact sheet for asset inspection. It is a
Blender studio render, not evidence of Godot gameplay or target-device speed.
The manifest records the generator version, source SHA-256, each GLB SHA-256,
triangle count, materials, footprint and geometric bounds. Do not manually edit
these measurements. Regenerate the collection after changing the source.
`tools/check_models.py` runs without Blender and checks source/asset hashes,
embedded geometry, actual transformed bounds, catalog footprints, named character
pivots, appearance materials and the current triangle/mesh budgets.

The 47-model collection includes all 31 placeable furnishing kinds, four resident hair
variants, linden tree, shrub, street lantern, mailbox, fence, flower patch,
tomato/radish crops, three window styles and an open door frame. Floor and wall
runs are generated from the actual house layout by the renderer.

The construction expansion adds five seats, six light fixtures, a straight oak
staircase and five landscaping models. They are original geometry from the same
editable Blender source, with the same project provenance and LFS rules. Each
has an explicit `custom_` material for the existing recoloring palette. Lamp
illumination is supplied by Godot lights at catalog-defined positions, ranges
and colors; the GLBs themselves do not carry lights.

Catalog pictures are runtime studio renders of these exact GLBs in one reusable
160×120 SubViewport. They are memory-cached UI textures, not substitute world
models or committed binary thumbnails. Floor patterns, wallpaper, wall openings,
diagonal wall segments, terrain and pools are editable procedural geometry in
`game/lot_scene_3d.gd`; their layout comes only from authoritative commands.

## Scale, orientation and names

| Contract | Required convention |
| --- | --- |
| Units | One metre per simulation tile; retain catalog footprint dimensions |
| Axes | glTF / Godot Y up, X across, Z depth |
| Front | +Z is the open/use side; bed headboard and sofa back are at -Z |
| Root origin | Center of the unrotated footprint at ground height Y=0 |
| Rotation | Apply the renderer's quarter turn around Y; never bake a placed rotation |
| Export | Self-contained `.glb`, applied scale, baked bevels/normals, no camera or lights |
| Furniture path | `assets/models/<catalog_kind>.glb` |
| Recoloring | Only material names beginning `custom_` receive the furniture swatch |
| Appearance | Resident materials `skin`, `hair`, `shirt`, `trousers`, `shoes` |
| Dynamic surfaces | `canvas` for an easel image, `soil` for garden moisture, `fruit` for ripeness |

Leave a small margin inside the footprint. Handles, soft cushions, window sills
and leaves may extend a few centimetres where they cannot imply another usable
tile. A linden canopy is deliberately wider than its trunk placement. Roots and
planter soil are allowed small deliberate below-ground portions. Furniture must
never acquire invisible collision from decorative mesh detail: the existing
catalog footprints and build rules remain the simulation authority.

Residents are approximately 1.74m high. A 1×2 bed is 0.96m wide and 1.93m long,
with a mattress top around 0.55m. Sofa cushions top out around 0.58m; dining/game
table tops are around 0.83m. Use these dimensions when posing activities. Do not
scale a model independently to hide a pose or click-target error.

The four resident GLBs share direct root pivots `Torso`, `Head`, `Arm_L`, `Arm_R`,
`Leg_L`, and `Leg_R`. Head rotation starts at the neck (Y=1.30m); shoulder pivots
are at Y=1.20m and hips at Y=0.755m. Their mesh children hold geometry in pivot-local
coordinates. Use the actual mesh bounds for picking and head labels, not the
pivot origin. Hair is chosen with `resident.glb`, `resident_bob.glb`,
`resident_curls.glb`, or `resident_shaved.glb`. Do not rename these pivots or merge
meshes across them. Walk, seated and sleeping poses are presentation state, not
saved gameplay state.

## Shape and material quality

Start with a recognizable silhouette: cushion and arm heights on a sofa, the
fridge's separated freezer, a toilet bowl and cistern, an easel's tripod, a CRT's
deep cabinet. Then add a few useful details such as seams, book spines, handles,
a lamp frame or a table setting. Details must stay readable at normal play zoom;
do not fill every surface with scratches or fine noise.

Use gentle 1–4cm bevels on manufactured edges and larger 4–9cm rounding on soft
cushions. Keep hard wood surfaces flat with weighted corner normals; round skin,
ceramics, metal tubes and leaves with restrained smoothing. The low-poly canopy
can retain facets, but a household object must not look accidentally faceted.
No paper-thin legs, mirrored text, fused fingers, floating parts or intersecting
assembly seams are acceptable.

Materials should sit in the existing muted warm palette. Oak and walnut anchor
the furniture; linen is warm cream, upholstery may be sage, dusty blue or
terracotta. Preserve leaf greens, glass, books and metal when a furnishing is
recolored. Surface roughness should dominate: painted metal and ceramic can be
slightly polished; no mirror-like furniture or uniformly metallic props. The
first collection uses solid materials with modeled details, keeping exports
self-contained and inexpensive to download. Transparent shower/glass surfaces
are represented with opaque frosted colors to avoid sorting defects.

Keep lighting outside the model. Avoid painted directional shadows, permanent
highlights that disagree with the game sun, baked black undersides, and
self-illuminated daytime furniture. A lantern's subtle emission is a recognizable
light source; its actual illumination is supplied by the scene.

## Cost and consistency

As a working budget, aim for 1,000–5,000 triangles and 3–8 material groups per
small furnishing. Bookshelves and game tables may reach roughly 12,000 triangles
where books or pieces justify it; residents currently use roughly 6,000–8,000
triangles and separate animated parts. Prefer reusing one imported resource per
kind, and merge static geometry by material. Do not combine limbs merely to meet
a draw-call count. Any increase needs an in-game comparison at a furnished lot,
not just an attractive studio render.

Review a new asset beside the bed, sofa, plant and resident under the same light
and orthographic camera. Check all four rotations, original and all five alternate
furniture colors, daylight and night, normal/compact windows, and the lowest
scenery detail setting. Walk a resident around it and use it. Verify feet and
legs meet the floor, occupants rest on the actual cushion or mattress, and the
visible mesh selects the intended object. Review build placement, move and room
copy previews as well as completed placement. Save/reload must not change the
look or introduce another gameplay footprint.

## Adding outside or generated models

For an outside asset, require an explicit CC0 grant from its creator's own
download page or included license. Keep the original URL, creator, download date,
archive checksum, license text and changes made in the asset provenance. A search
result's “free” label is insufficient. Normalize scale, front direction, origin,
material names and palette before integration. Reject attractive assets that do
not fit the collection rather than assembling several unrelated styles.

A 2D-to-3D output is a draft, even if its source illustration looks polished.
Inspect the back and underside, open holes, thin surfaces, normals, duplicate
faces, UVs and topology. Retopologize where needed, separate articulated parts,
correct scale, and provide editable source and generator/model provenance.
Never ship an uninspected generated mesh or infer that a service's output is CC0.

## Git LFS

Binary asset files must be Git LFS objects, including `.glb`, source `.blend`,
textures, audio, screenshots and packaged source archives. Keep the generator,
manifest and prose as normal text. Install Git LFS before cloning or adding art.
After editing an asset, check the staged path with `git check-attr filter -- path`
and verify it reports `lfs`; then run `git lfs fsck`. Commit the actual hydrated
asset through the LFS filter, never a manually written pointer. See
[BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md) for repository setup and the history-migration handoff.


## Parametric creator residents

`game/painting_pose.gd` is the editable original source for the runtime solid oak
step and held paintbrush. It uses the existing resident arm pivot and unchanged
easel canvas face (local Z=0.134m) to solve hand/brush contact at each body height.
The support stays stationary through the stroke. Step surfaces batch by material;
step and brush together add at most five mesh instances to an active painter.
This source uses the repository MIT license, with no external assets or binary
export. Godot rebuilds the props when an accepted painter first arrives, and
profile changes rebuild the resident and props together. Run
`tests/painting_pose.gd` for the native rotation/height/day/night/compact review.
The Blender easel and existing character mesh geometry remain unchanged.

The runtime rig includes `Leg_L/Knee_L` and `Leg_R/Knee_R`. Straight legs retain
their original geometry and bounds; the two knee pivots allow dining feet to
point down. They add two mesh groups without increasing the measured triangle
budget. Activities reset both knee rotations before applying dining or TV seating poses.
TV seating reuses the existing catalog meshes and articulated resident rig;
`game/lot_scene_3d.gd` aligns the body to the accepted cushion/slat height.
`tests/television_pose.gd` reviews those contacts and native picking.

`game/dining_pose.gd` is the editable original source for runtime solid oak stools,
child footrests, plates and recipe-colored food. It regenerates these activity
props in Godot and batches their meshes by material. This source is covered by
the repository MIT license, uses no external assets and needs no binary export.
The existing dining-table GLB remains unchanged; its decorative settings hide
while actual reserved places are shown. Review with `tests/dining_pose.gd` in
the actual game camera, including adjacent diners, day/night and compact windows.

`game/character_model.gd` is a second editable original geometry master, covered
by the repository MIT license. It generates solid `ArrayMesh` surfaces directly
in Godot for detailed creator profiles. `character_studio.gd` supplies the review
room/camera, and `character_voice.gd` synthesizes an original nonverbal greeting.
No outside art, pretrained output, recorded voice or additional binary source is
used. Existing Blender-authored resident GLBs remain archived in the collection;
live residents now all use this generator, including legacy saves. Legacy appearance
colors and hair choices are projected without adding profiles to saved state. Do
not replace or re-export those unchanged GLBs when editing this generator.

Rebuild this asset by reopening the creator/changing a draft choice; the same
source regenerates neighborhood meshes on accepted profile changes. Body, head,
arms and legs retain the established named pivots. Geometry is merged by material
within each articulated part; eyes stay independent for blinks. Creator meshes
reach 32,268 triangles and world meshes 12,732 in the checked age/hair/body/outfit
matrix, with regression ceilings of 36,000 / 14,000 and at most ten mesh instances
per character. The world uses fewer head/loft segments and sampled curves. This
explicit detail exception serves the large creator camera and keeps distant
residents cheaper. Both detail levels retain the same silhouette and pivots. Shape
changes do not modify navigation footprints. Baby bassinets belong to the resident presentation and
are not independently purchasable furniture or pathfinding blockers.

Regeneration/review commands (screenshots stay in ignored `build/`):

```sh
python3 tools/godot.py --headless --script res://tests/character_models.gd
python3 tools/godot.py --script res://tests/character_art_review.gd
python3 tools/godot.py --script res://tests/character_creator_ui.gd
python3 tools/godot.py --script res://tests/character_creator_ui.gd -- --compact
python3 tools/godot.py --script res://tests/character_creator_ui.gd -- --compact --low
```

Inspect full body, face, back, wardrobe slots, child/baby/elder proportions, actual
neighborhood lighting and compact layout. Native images are `build/creator-*.png`.
The studio uses neutral daylight for faithful palette selection and a warmer
night comparison. Any screenshot or exported mesh added to Git still requires
LFS; this runtime pipeline itself produces no committed binaries.


### Sculpted resident direction — November 4, 2026

Use softly illustrated people with a continuous jaw, cheeks and nose bridge;
almond eyes with fitted lids; gently curved brows and a quiet mouth. Hair follows
the skull in broad combed masses, with distinct bob, long, gathered, braided and
textured silhouettes. Keep shoulders covered by their sleeves, wrists tapered,
cloth hems/collars/pockets legible, and footwear grounded. Avoid exposed spherical
eyeballs, bead noses, ball-joint elbows and stacked ellipsoids as a hairstyle.

The indexed mesh helpers author smooth normals around seams. Hair, face details,
collars and hems remain actual geometry. Small lids/brows/lips do not cast
oversized shadow-map artifacts; the head and hair still cast real shadows. The
creator uses bounded studio light energy so pale skin and silver hair keep their
color. No painted highlights, raster textures or new external sources are used.

`tests/character_art_review.gd` produces native contact sheets for every hairstyle,
all six skin/hair colors, face/nose variation, all seven outfit occasions,
accessories and reverse evening angles. They supplement, rather than replace,
the creator UI, furnished neighborhood and movement/picking checks. Review both
mesh detail levels; creator close-ups alone cannot establish gameplay quality.


### Procedural floor and wall joins

Exterior wall-cell floor surfaces end at the wall footprint, including corners;
open floor/deck tiles keep their whole tile. The hidden cutaway ceiling reaches
wall faces at wall-top height so gaps cannot create false interior sunlight
bands. Lower-storey walls meet the next floor slab underside. These solids are
original runtime geometry from `game/lot_scene_3d.gd`; regenerate by reopening
the scene. No Blender or binary export is needed. Run
`tests/floor_wall_render.gd` for matched day/night and compact native review;
see [floor/wall QA](FLOOR_WALL_QA.md) for evidence and diagonal-floor limits.


Near/interior doorways follow the low wall cutaway with solid low jamb ends and
an open threshold. The unchanged `door_frame.glb` remains the full rear/raised
model and a hidden shadow caster in the cutaway view. The three procedural boxes
are original MIT-licensed geometry in `game/lot_scene_3d.gd`; rebuild by reopening
the scene, with no binary export. `tests/world_polish.gd` reviews normal/compact
day/dusk/night views and rear/side/diagonal frames. See [world QA](WORLD_POLISH_QA.md).
